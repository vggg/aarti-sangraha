# Where the booklet differs from your photographed source

Per your decision (17 Sep 2026): **standard/common published text throughout**, with your ten
photographs used as the cross-check rather than the authority. Every departure is listed here.
Nothing else was changed.

Legend — **Book** = what your photographed Aarti Sangrah page prints. **Booklet** = what this
edition prints.

## 1. श्री गणपतीची आरती  (photo IMG_09F88790)

| Line | Book | Booklet | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| GAN-002 | जायची | **जयाची** | Book form is not a Marathi word here; जयाची is universal. |
| GAN-003 | सर्वांग | **सर्वांगी** | Metre needs the -ी; all standard printings have it. |
| GAN-004 | मुक्ताफळाची, no verse number | **मुक्ताफळांची ॥१॥** | Anusvara restored; verse 1 marker restored (your page omits it and jumps to ॥२॥). |
| GAN-005 | फार तुज | **फरा तुज** | फरा = the jewelled canopy/crest. फार ("a lot") breaks the sense. |
| GAN-006 | चान्दांची | **चंदनाची** | चंदन = sandalwood, paired with कुंकुम-केशर. |
| GAN-007 | हिरेजडीत मुगुट शोभती बरा | **हिरेजडित मुकुट शोभतो बरा** | मुकुट = crown; शोभतो agrees with it. |
| GAN-008 | नुपुरे | **नूपुरे** | Long ū is standard. |
| GAN-009 | पितांबर फणीवरबंधना | **पीतांबर फणिवरवंदना** | पीत = yellow. वंदना (salutation) not बंधना. |
| GAN-010 | **तोंड** | **सोंड** | सोंड = trunk, paired with वक्रतुंड. तोंड ("mouth") is the single largest divergence in your book. |
| GAN-R02 | मनःकामना | **मनकामना** | Ramdas's form; both circulate, standard printings use मनकामना. |
| GAN-R01 | जयदेव जयदेव (joined) | **जय देव जय देव** | Word spacing only. |
